Which e-books are available here?

The Trucking Terminology Glossary covers general trucking, dispatch, and Safety & Compliance vocabulary. The IFTA + IRP Handbook organizes references to offices, forms, documents, and processes across 48 U.S. states. New titles will join the catalog as they are published.

What language are the e-books in?

Both books in this collection are in Spanish and include English terms and acronyms. You can browse the catalog in English or Spanish; changing the page language does not change the language of the file.

Is an e-book the same as a course?

An e-book is a digital reference book. Courses are separate training programs with their own content and terms. Visit each product page to review exactly what it includes.

Where can I review each book’s terms?

Select “View e-book” to visit its product page and review acquisition and delivery information. The current product pages are in Spanish. This catalog lets you first review the contents, format, page count, and file language.
