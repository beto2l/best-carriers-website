Frequently asked questions

What to know before you begin

Is the course live?

No. This is recorded training with immediate access, so you can start and learn at your own pace.

Do I need a USDOT number to take the course?

Not necessarily. The course explains processes for existing companies as well as setup and access concepts useful to professionals who work with clients.

Is the course useful if I work with clients as a tax preparer or TSP?

Yes. The curriculum includes processes relevant to preparers, compliance staff and Transportation Service Providers, including registration as a Transportation Service Provider, Designate TSP and Authorized Users.

When can I start?

You receive access after payment approval and can learn at your own pace.

Does the course include individual help with my case?

No. Individual consulting is optional, purchased separately and costs an additional $150.

Does it include a certificate?

Yes. You may receive a certificate after completing the required course content.

Where do I receive support?

Included support is provided through a private Facebook group. Access is delivered to students after purchase.

What should I prepare before starting?

It is helpful to have access to the email related to your registrations and, if you are working with an existing company, its company information and USDOT number. Each lesson explains what is needed for that process.

What happens if FMCSA changes MOTUS?

Access includes future updates that Best Carriers adds to this training to reflect relevant system changes.

Can I request a refund?

No. Because this is digital access, all sales are final. Review the terms before purchasing.

Is Best Carriers part of FMCSA?

No. Best Carriers is an independent educational brand. MOTUS, FMCSA, USDOT and Login.gov belong to their respective owners.
