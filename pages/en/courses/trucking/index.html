BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Clear answers to your questions.

Is this trucking webinar really free?

Yes. The official SBDC listing states there is no fee. Registration takes place on its website. Best Carriers does not process payments for this event.

When and where does it take place?

Tuesday, September 29, 2026, from 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. CDT, Texas and Chicago time. It is a live online session. Check access instructions directly with SBDC when registering.

Do I need to own a truck or a company?

The webinar is intended for entrepreneurs who want to start a trucking business. Use this introduction to organize your ideas, even if you are still exploring which vehicle or type of operation interests you.

Is it relevant for a cargo van, hotshot or box truck business?

Yes: the focus is understanding the foundations of a transportation business. It also welcomes people considering dry van, flatbed, reefer, dump truck or towing truck operations. It is not an exhaustive technical course for each vehicle type.

Is the webinar in English or Spanish?

The advertised session is in Spanish. This English page helps you review the event; it does not represent a separate English-language session.

What will I learn about startup permits and costs?

The published agenda includes necessary permits and approximate startup costs. How they apply to a company depends on its operation. The session helps identify topics to review and does not replace an individual assessment.

Who organizes and teaches the session?

Sul Ross State University International SBDC organizes the event. Alberto Ibarra participates as a Best Carriers instructor. SBDC manages registration and event instructions.

Does it include a recording, certificate or Best Carriers course access?

The official information reviewed does not confirm a recording or certificate for this webinar. Check with SBDC. Free registration does not include access to paid Best Carriers courses.
